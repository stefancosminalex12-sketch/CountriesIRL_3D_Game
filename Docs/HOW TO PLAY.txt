Crowns & Commoners - v0.1.0 pre-alpha test build
==========================================

Wars of the Roses, England, 1455. Everyone is a countryball.
This is an early test: a small practice area, not the real game world yet.

HOW TO START
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop).
2. Double-click  CountriesIRL_3D_Game.exe, then choose New Game on the title screen
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the game is not signed yet).
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway". This is normal for small indie test builds.
4. If Windows asks to install "Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable" or DirectX, allow it.

CONTROLS
  W A S D        move (on a horse: steer where you look)
  Mouse          look around
  Shift (hold)   run / gallop on a horse (uses stamina)
  On a horse     W walks; Shift: tap = trot (free), hold = canter,
                 double-tap and hold = gallop (canter and gallop use stamina)
  Space          jump (the horse jumps too)
  Left click     punch
  Right click    hold to raise your guard
  E              get on / off a horse (stand close to it)
  V              switch first-person / third-person view
  T              change your eyes' emotion (just for fun)
  Tab / I        equipment (being built)
  Esc            menu: resume, controls, main menu, quit

WHAT TO TRY
- Walk up to the two horses (white and brown), press E. Hold W to walk, tap Shift to trot,
  hold Shift to canter, double-tap and hold Shift to gallop.
- Punch the other countryballs. Defeated ones stay, slowly decay, and end as bones.
- The day passes in 48 minutes, with a real medieval calendar, seasons and English weather.

NEEDS
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a reasonably modern graphics card (DirectX 12).

Please send feedback: what felt good, what felt wrong, any crashes (and what you were doing).
